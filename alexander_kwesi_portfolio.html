About Me

Passionate educator
with a technical edge.

I am Alexander Oluwaseun Kwesi, an enthusiastic educator based in Warrington, Cheshire. My teaching philosophy centres on making complex subjects accessible, engaging, and relevant — whether I am guiding young scholars through Python fundamentals or supporting primary students in core subjects.

My dual background in computing (MSc from the University of South Wales) and hands-on classroom experience allows me to bridge the gap between technical instruction and effective pedagogy. I believe every learner has unique potential, and I am committed to creating environments where curiosity and confidence flourish.

Currently completing my TEFL Academy Diploma, I am expanding my reach to EFL contexts while continuing to develop resources for digital literacy and computational thinking.
